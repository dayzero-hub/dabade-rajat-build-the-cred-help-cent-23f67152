Frequently asked questions

A payment is usually declined by your bank, not by us — an expired card, an exhausted limit, or an international block on the card. Check with your bank, then retry from the payments screen.

Coins are credited once the bank confirms your payment, which is normally within a few minutes. During a bank outage it can take up to 48 hours.

Banks post payments on their own schedule, so the outstanding amount can lag by up to three working days. If it has been longer than that, share the transaction reference with support.

Different bureaus score you with different models and refresh on different dates. We show the Experian score, refreshed monthly, so a gap against another bureau is expected.
